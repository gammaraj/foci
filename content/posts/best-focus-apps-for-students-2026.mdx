Students do not need another complicated productivity suite. They need a **timer that starts in seconds**, a **short task list**, and **sound that masks dorm or café noise** — without another subscription.

We ranked free focus apps for studying in 2026 on friction, features, and whether they survive a real exam week.

## What students actually need from a focus app

- **Instant start** — no mandatory account before the first session
- **Tasks + timer together** — so you know what the session is for
- **Ambient sound** — brown noise or lo-fi without juggling YouTube
- **Session goals / streaks** — visible progress across a semester
- **Works on laptop and phone** — browser or PWA is enough for most people

## 1. Foci — Best overall for students

**Price:** Free · **Platform:** Web / PWA  
**Best for:** Studying with a timer, task list, and brown noise in one tab

Foci combines Pomodoro (and Flowtime / 52/17 presets), per-task time logging, daily goals, and offline ambient sounds. No signup required for guest use; optional account syncs streaks across devices.

**Why it ranks #1 for students:** less app-switching during study blocks, and it works for ADHD-friendly external structure without a paywall.

## 2. Pomofocus — Best minimal timer

**Price:** Free (ads) / Premium  
**Best for:** Students who only want a clean countdown

Simple and fast. Weak on ambient sound and deeper task tracking.

## 3. Forest — Best gamified phone blocker

**Price:** Paid on mobile  
**Best for:** Students who grow virtual trees to stay off their phone

Motivating, but light on tasks and desktop workflows. See [Foci vs Forest](/blog/foci-vs-forest-app) and [Forest alternatives](/blog/forest-app-alternatives).

## 4. Focus To-Do — Best if you already live in a task list

**Price:** Free + premium  
**Best for:** Students who want Pomodoro glued to a to-do app

Solid hybrid, but heavier than a browser-first system for many people.

## 5. Tide — Best premium soundscapes

**Price:** Freemium  
**Best for:** Students who care most about polished ambient audio

Strong sounds; weaker as an all-in-one study system.
