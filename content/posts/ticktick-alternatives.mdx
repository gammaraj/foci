> **Evergreen summary:** [Best TickTick alternatives](/alternatives/ticktick)

TickTick is strong as a combined to-do list, calendar, and Pomodoro timer. People leave because Premium gates the pieces they actually use, the app feels busy, or they only needed a focus session with a task list.

Here are the alternatives that come up most often — and which problem each one actually solves.

## Why people look for TickTick alternatives

- **Premium paywall** — extra lists, calendar views, and timer options sit behind a subscription
- **Calendar-first UI** — noisy if you just wanted today’s tasks and a timer
- **Habits you do not use** — you wanted focus, not another tracker
- **Web as a second-class client** — you work in the browser all day

If any of those are the reason, pick the alternative that matches the leftover job.

---

## 1. Foci — Best overall free alternative

**Price:** Free  
**Platform:** Web (any device), installable as PWA  
**Best for:** Tasks + timer + ambient sound in one tab

Foci is the closest free substitute when you opened TickTick to work, not to manage a calendar. Projects, due dates, Pomodoro/Flowtime presets, Smart Plan, and offline brown noise.

**Why it replaces TickTick for many people:**
- No Premium wall for timer, lists, or streaks
- Start at [usefoci.com/app](https://usefoci.com/app) without an account
- Smart Plan when the list will not fit today
- Sharing with viewer/editor roles if you collaborate

**Skip it if:** you need TickTick’s habit grid or a week-agenda home screen.

See the side-by-side: [Foci vs TickTick](/vs/ticktick).

---

## 2. Todoist — Best task manager (no timer)

**Price:** Free tier; Pro paid  
**Best for:** Capture, filters, and GTD-style organization

If you outgrew TickTick’s lists and never used the Pomodoro, Todoist is the usual landing place. It does not include a focus timer or ambient sound. Pair it with Foci if you still want sessions.

Import path if you switch to Foci: [Migrate from Todoist](/blog/migrate-from-todoist-to-foci).

---

## 3. Microsoft To Do — Best if you live in Outlook

**Price:** Free with a Microsoft account  
**Best for:** My Day + Outlook/365

Simple lists, My Day, and Outlook tasks. No serious Pomodoro, no Smart Plan, no built-in brown noise. Fine if Microsoft 365 is already the OS of your work.

---

## 4. Stay on TickTick

Keep TickTick if the **calendar + habits + lists** combo is how you run the week and Premium is cheap relative to that workflow. Alternatives are worse at being TickTick.
