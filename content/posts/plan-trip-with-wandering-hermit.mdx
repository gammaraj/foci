Trip planning looks fun until you open seventeen browser tabs, compare flights for two hours, and still have not booked anything. The problem is rarely the itinerary — it is **sustained focus** on boring but essential tasks.

This guide pairs two free tools from our product family:

- **[Wandering Hermit](https://www.wanderinghermit.com/?utm_source=foci&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=blog)** — AI trip planner plus curated day-by-day guides for Europe and Asia
- **Foci** — Pomodoro timer, tasks, Smart Plan, and a **Trip Planning** task template

Plan the route on Wandering Hermit. Execute the prep in focused sessions on Foci.

## Step 1: Build your itinerary on Wandering Hermit

Start at [wanderinghermit.com](https://www.wanderinghermit.com/?utm_source=foci&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=blog):

1. Enter your destinations and trip length (up to 14 days).
2. Generate a custom AI itinerary **or** browse a curated guide (France, Japan, Portugal, Spain & Portugal, and more).
3. Email the plan to yourself or keep it open in a tab.

You now have the **what** — days, cities, and activities. Foci handles the **when you actually do the work**.

## Step 2: Load tasks into Foci

Open [Foci](https://usefoci.com/app?utm_source=wanderinghermit&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=blog) and choose one of three paths:

### Option A — Trip Planning template (fastest)

1. Open the task panel menu → **Templates**.
2. Select **Trip Planning ✈️**.
3. Foci creates nine pre-built tasks (visa research, flights, hotels, itinerary review, etc.).

### Option B — Import CSV from Wandering Hermit

On any Wandering Hermit itinerary page or after generating an AI plan, click **Download planning tasks (CSV)**. Then in Foci:

1. Settings → **Import & Export Tasks**
2. Upload the CSV
3. Tasks appear with titles like “Review itinerary — Day 3: Kyoto temples”

### Option C — Manual list

Add tasks yourself: one Pomodoro per booking decision beats an unstructured “plan trip tonight” block.

## Step 3: Run Pomodoro sessions on real work

Assign one task per focus block:

| Task | Suggested timer |
|------|-----------------|
| Compare flights | Classic Pomodoro (25 min) |
| Review full itinerary | Deep Work (50 min) |
| Book hotels for 3 cities | 2–3 Pomodoros |
| Print cheat sheet / insurance | Short Sprint (15 min) |

Select a task in Foci, turn on brown noise or lo-fi if it helps, and press **Start**. When the timer ends, take the break — then pick the next booking task.

Use **Smart Plan** if tasks have due dates: Foci schedules them across the days before departure.

## Why this pairing works

- **Wandering Hermit** reduces decision fatigue on *where to go* and *what to do each day*.
- **Foci** reduces decision fatigue on *when to do the admin* — flights, insurance, reservations.
- Together they separate **creative planning** from **execution**, which is how most people actually finish trip prep.

## Quick checklist before you leave

- [ ] Itinerary saved or emailed from Wandering Hermit
- [ ] Flights and main hotels booked
- [ ] Inter-city transport reserved where needed
- [ ] Language cheat sheet downloaded ([French](https://www.wanderinghermit.com/en/cheatsheets/french-travel?utm_source=foci&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=blog), [Spanish](https://www.wanderinghermit.com/en/cheatsheets/spanish-travel?utm_source=foci&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=blog), etc.)
- [ ] Travel insurance and emergency contacts noted
- [ ] One final Pomodoro to re-read Days 1–2
